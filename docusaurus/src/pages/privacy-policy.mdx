**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026

This page explains what data Livo collects, why, and how you can control it. Livo is a
personal media player that plays your own M3U and Xtream Codes playlists and media sources
on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Android. The app does not provide IPTV content, channels, or
subscriptions to content, and it never sells or shares your playlists with third parties.

## 1. Data We Do Not Collect

Livo has no user accounts. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone
number, contacts, precise location, or payment details.

- **Playlist credentials:** IPTV usernames and passwords are stored on your device only
  (in the iOS Keychain or the Android equivalent) and are masked in logs and crash reports.
- **Continue Watching and settings sync:** on Apple devices this is stored in your own
  iCloud account, which we cannot access.
- **Purchases:** premium subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google
  (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

## 2. Data Collected by Third-Party Services

Livo uses the following services. They collect anonymous device and usage data that is not
linked to your identity.

- **Firebase Analytics:** usage statistics such as screens visited, playback events and
  app settings (for example language, theme and subscription state), plus device model, OS
  version and approximate region.
- **Firebase Crashlytics:** crash reports and diagnostic data, with an anonymous
  installation identifier.
- **Firebase Performance Monitoring:** app performance data such as start-up and network
  timings.
- **Firebase Remote Config:** fetches app configuration; it uses an anonymous installation
  identifier.
- **Google AdMob** (iPhone, iPad and Android; there are no ads on Apple TV and no ads for
  premium users): ad impressions and clicks, approximate location derived from your IP
  address, and, only if you allow it, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA/AAID).

These services process data under their own privacy policies:
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 3. Data Sent to Livo's Own Servers

Some features talk to our own servers (`api.livoplayer.com`, `qr.livoplayer.com`):

- **Movie and series information:** to show posters, descriptions and cast, the app sends
  the title of the content you open. Results are cached on your device.
- **Secure playback links:** some streams use plain, unencrypted HTTP. For these, the app
  sends the stream address to our server to get a secure playback link.
- **QR pairing (Apple TV):** when you add a playlist to Apple TV by scanning a QR code, the
  playlist details you enter on your phone pass through our server only to deliver them to
  your Apple TV.

We use this data only to provide these features. We do not use it for advertising or
analytics, and we do not sell or share it.

## 4. Your Choices

- **Analytics opt-out:** turn off **Settings > Privacy > Share Anonymous Usage Data** to
  stop sending usage statistics to Firebase Analytics.
- **Ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** on first launch Livo shows Google's consent
  form before any ad loads. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Settings > Privacy > Ad Privacy Choices**.
- **App Tracking Transparency (iOS and iPadOS):** Livo asks for permission before AdMob may
  use your advertising identifier. Livo works the same whether you allow it or not, and you
  can change this at any time in the system settings
  (**Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**).
- **Android:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

## 5. Purpose of Processing

We use the collected data to understand which features are used, find and fix crashes,
keep the app fast, configure the app remotely, and show ads to users who do not have
premium.

## 6. Data Retention

Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the retention periods set in Firebase and
AdMob. Data on your device is deleted when you remove the app.

## 7. Children

Livo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

## 8. IPTV Content

Livo hosts no IPTV content and offers no channels. All content is provided by the user,
and the user is fully responsible for the content they add.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the latest
version.

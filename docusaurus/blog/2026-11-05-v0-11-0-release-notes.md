Livo Player v0.11.0 for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV adds a TV guide for live channels, playback speed for movies and series, series that open at your next episode, grouped quality variants and a reworked remote experience on Apple TV.

<!-- truncate -->

v0.10.0 rebuilt the foundation. v0.11.0 builds on it with the feature many of you asked for most, a TV guide, and with a lot of polish on Apple TV, where moving around with the remote should now feel predictable everywhere.

## TV guide

- See **what's on now and next** on Live channel cards and in the live player, with a programme timeline.
- On Apple TV, the Live screen gets a **programme preview panel** for the focused channel.
- The guide comes from your playlist's own guide address, or from one you enter in Settings. Xtream Codes sources fall back to their own guide.

## Player

- **Playback speed** for movies and series, from 0.5× to 2×.
- Your **default audio and subtitle languages** from Settings are applied as soon as playback starts.
- The **audio codec** is shown in the track lists and in the info panel.
- On Apple TV, press on the progress bar to play or pause, and scrubbing is smoother.
- Closing the player that you opened from Continue Watching takes you back to Home.

## Live TV

- **Quality variants of the same channel** (HD, FHD, 4K) are grouped into one card.
- **Smoother live quality switching**: the picture holds during a switch, and repeated buffering moves to a lower quality faster.
- On Apple TV, Live gets new **All** and **Favorites** lists.
- Fixed a wrong live delay after a live stream restarts.

## Movies and series

- **Series open at your next episode**, and Continue Watching shows the season and episode.
- **Recently Added shows one card per series**, with a long-press menu for Watchlist and Watched.
- On Apple TV, the series grid, seasons and episodes can all be navigated with the remote, and Top Shelf names episodes as "Series • S01 • E03".

## Apple TV remote

- **Remote navigation was reworked** across Home, Live, Movies, Series and the player: focus stays where you left it, and the Menu button always steps back.

## Reliability

- Many smaller bug fixes across iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

v0.11.0 is now rolling out on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6755977918?ct=blog-v0-11-0&mt=8) for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. If you don't see the update yet, open Livo's page on the App Store and tap Update. As always, Livo plays your own M3U and Xtream Codes playlists and does not provide any content or channels.

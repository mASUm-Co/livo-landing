# Livo Player documentation

Livo Player is a video player for the IPTV playlists you already have. Add an M3U playlist or an Xtream Codes account and Livo organizes it into Live TV, Movies and Series, with a TV guide, Continue Watching and a full-featured player.

:::info Livo does not provide content
Livo does not sell or include any channels, streams or IPTV subscriptions. You need your own playlist from your provider.
:::

## Supported devices

| Platform | Devices | Minimum version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| iOS and iPadOS | iPhone, iPad | iOS 16.0 |
| tvOS | Apple TV | tvOS 17.6 |
| Android | Phones, tablets, Android TV / Google TV | Android 7.0 |

The app is available in English, Turkish, Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Russian and Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

## Where to start

1. [Install and open Livo](/docs/getting-started/install-and-open)
2. Add your source: [M3U playlist](/docs/getting-started/add-m3u-playlist) or [Xtream Codes account](/docs/getting-started/add-xtream-account)
3. Start watching: [Live TV](/docs/usage/live-tv), [TV guide](/docs/usage/tv-guide), [Movies and series](/docs/usage/movies-and-series)

## Main features

- **Live TV** with channel groups, favorites, quality variants grouped into one card and automatic quality switching
- **TV guide (EPG)**: what is on now and next, on channel cards and in the live player
- **Movies and series** with posters, details, a Technical section, watched states and a watchlist
- **Continue Watching** that resumes where you stopped and opens series at your next episode
- **A real player**: two playback engines, audio and subtitle tracks, playback speed, Picture in Picture and AirPlay on Apple devices, Cast on Android
- **Apple TV**: full remote navigation, QR pairing to add a source from your phone, Top Shelf
- **Premium**: no ads, sync across devices, AirPlay / Cast and sidebar customization. See [Premium](/docs/premium).
- **Privacy**: your playlists and logins stay on your device. See [Privacy and settings](/docs/privacy-and-settings).

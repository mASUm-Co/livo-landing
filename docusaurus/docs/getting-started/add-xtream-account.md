# Add an Xtream Codes account

1. Open **IPTV Sources** and tap **+**.
2. Enter a **Display Name**.
3. Choose **Xtream Codes** as the **Type**.
4. Enter the **Server URL** exactly as your provider gave it, including the port, for example `http://host:port`.
5. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
6. Keep **Active source** on and tap **Save**.

Livo loads live channels, movies and series from the account. Xtream Codes accounts also provide their own guide, which Livo uses when no other guide address is set. See [TV guide](/docs/usage/tv-guide).

Your username and password are stored securely on your device (in the Keychain on Apple devices). They are never included in analytics or logs.

On Apple TV and Android TV you can also add the account from your phone with a QR code, see [Add an M3U playlist](/docs/getting-started/add-m3u-playlist#on-apple-tv-and-android-tv-add-with-qr).

## Common issues

- The server URL is missing `http://` or the port.
- The username or password has a typo, or the account has expired.
- The provider's server is temporarily down.

See [Login or credential errors](/docs/troubleshooting/login-errors).

# FAQ

## Does Livo provide channels?

No. Livo is only a player for sources you add. You need your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes account.

## Does Livo sell IPTV subscriptions?

No. Livo Premium is a subscription for app features (no ads, sync, AirPlay / Cast, sidebar customization). It does not include channels or content.

## Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad (iOS 16 or later), Apple TV (tvOS 17.6 or later) and Android phones, tablets and TVs (Android 7.0 or later).

## Does Livo have a TV guide?

Yes. Livo shows the guide from your playlist, from an XMLTV address you enter, or from your Xtream Codes account. See [TV guide](/docs/usage/tv-guide).

## Why does a channel appear only once although my playlist has SD, HD and 4K versions?

Livo groups quality variants of the same channel into one card. Choose the quality in the player, or leave it on automatic. See [Live TV](/docs/usage/live-tv).

## Can I use one playlist on several devices?

Yes, add it on each device. Your provider may limit how many streams can play at the same time. With Premium, Continue Watching and settings sync between your devices.

## Are my logins safe?

Your playlists and logins are stored on your device and never sent to analytics. See [Privacy and settings](/docs/privacy-and-settings).

## How do I restore Premium on a new device?

Choose **Restore Purchases** in **Settings → Subscription**. See [Premium](/docs/premium).

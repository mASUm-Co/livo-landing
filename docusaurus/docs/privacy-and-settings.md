# Privacy and settings

## Your data

- Your playlists, Xtream logins and guide addresses are stored on your device. Logins are kept in the device's secure storage (Keychain on Apple devices).
- Livo does not sell or share your playlists or logins. Stream addresses and credentials are never included in analytics or logs.
- No sign-up is needed.

## What goes through livoplayer.com

To play your streams and show details, Livo uses its own server, livoplayer.com, for:

- **Checking a stream's format** before playback. Plain `http://` addresses you added are passed through the server so they can be opened securely; the server does not provide or store content.
- **Movie and series details** such as posters and overviews.
- **QR pairing** on TVs: the details you type on your phone are passed to the TV.

## Privacy settings

**Settings → Privacy**:

- **Share Anonymous Usage Data**: anonymous statistics (screens visited, playback events) that help improve Livo. Turn it off to stop sending them.
- **Ad Privacy Choices**: review or change your ad consent. On iPhone and iPad, ad tracking also follows **App Tracking Transparency** in the system Settings.

Premium members see no ads.

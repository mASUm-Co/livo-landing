# Appearance and language

## Theme

<DocScreenshot shot="tv-08-settings" alt="Livo settings on Apple TV" caption="Settings on Apple TV" />

**Settings → Appearance**: choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**. Auto follows your device's system setting.

## App language

**Settings → Language → App Language**. Livo is available in:

- English
- Turkish
- Spanish (Spain)
- Spanish (Mexico)
- Russian
- Chinese (Simplified)
- Chinese (Traditional)

## Subtitle appearance

**Settings → Subtitle Appearance** lets you set the font, size, text color, background color and opacity, outline and vertical position, with a live preview. On Apple TV, subtitles follow the system caption settings (**Settings → Accessibility → Subtitles and Captioning** on the Apple TV).

## Dates and times

Programme times and dates follow your device's region, time zone and 12/24-hour setting.

# Watch live TV

Open **Live** from Home. Channels are listed by the groups in your playlist.

## Groups, All and Favorites

- Pick a group in the **Groups** sidebar to see its channels. **All** lists every channel and **Favorites** lists the channels you saved.
- Long-press a channel (on Apple TV, press and hold the touch surface) and choose **Add to Favorites** or **Remove from Favorites**.
- Use the search field to find a channel by name.
- Premium members can hide groups and reorder lists in the sidebar.

## One card per channel, all qualities inside

Many playlists list the same channel several times, for example `SD`, `HD`, `FHD` and `4K`. Livo groups these quality variants into a single channel card, so your list stays short. When you play the channel, the **Quality** menu in the player lists every variant.

## Automatic quality

**Automatic Stream Quality** is on by default (**Settings → Playback**). Livo starts with a quality that suits your connection and moves between the channel's variants as conditions change. If playback keeps buffering it moves to a lower quality, and it tries a higher one again once the stream is stable. The picture stays on screen during a switch.

If you pick a quality yourself in the **Quality** menu, Livo keeps that quality for the channel and stops switching automatically.

## TV guide on channels

When a guide is available, channel cards show the programme that is on now, how much time is left and what is up next. On Apple TV a preview panel shows the selected channel's schedule. See [TV guide](/docs/usage/tv-guide).

## In the live player

- Use **Previous Channel** and **Next Channel** to change channels without leaving the player. In the channel list on iPhone and iPad, swipe left or right to switch quickly.
- The player shows the running programme with a timeline when a guide is available.
- If you pause a live stream or fall behind, the player shows how far behind live you are. Choose **Live** to jump back to the live edge.
- If a stream fails, Livo retries automatically and falls back to its second playback engine when needed. You can also choose **Retry**.

See [The player](/docs/usage/player) for audio, subtitles and other controls.

# Apple TV and the remote

Livo is built for the Siri Remote on Apple TV.

## Moving around

- Swipe or use the arrows to move focus. Focus stays where you left it when you go back to a screen.
- Press **Back** (**Menu** on older remotes) to step back one level: from the player to the list, from a list to its groups, and from there to Home.
- Press and hold on a card for quick actions such as **Add to Favorites**, **Add to Watchlist** and **Mark as watched**.

## Live

- The sidebar lists **All**, **Favorites** and your playlist's groups.
- Moving to a channel opens a preview panel with its current programme and today's schedule, when a guide is available.

## Series

Browse the series grid, then seasons and episodes with the remote. A series opens at your next episode.

## Player

- Press the center of the clickpad (or the touch surface) on the progress bar to play or pause.
- Movies and series: with the controls hidden, press left or right to skip 10 seconds back or forward. Swipe on the progress bar to scrub.
- Live: use the channel up/down buttons, if your remote has them, to change channels.
- Select the info button in the player controls for the info panel with **Info**, **Audio & Subtitles**, **Speed** and **Technical** tabs.
- During the up-next countdown, press **Back** to cancel it.

## Top Shelf

Put Livo in the top row of the Apple TV Home Screen to see your Continue Watching items in Top Shelf. Episodes are shown as *Series • S01 • E03*.

## Add a source from your phone

On the Apple TV, choose **Add IPTV Source With QR**, scan the code and type the playlist or Xtream details on your phone. See [Add an M3U playlist](/docs/getting-started/add-m3u-playlist#on-apple-tv-and-android-tv-add-with-qr).

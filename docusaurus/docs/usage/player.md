# The player

## Two playback engines

Livo plays most streams with the device's own player. If a stream uses a format that player cannot open, Livo switches to a second engine (VLC) automatically. HLS, MP4, MKV and most common stream formats play out of the box.

## Audio and subtitles

- Use the audio and subtitle buttons (on Apple TV, the info panel's **Audio & Subtitles** tab) to change tracks. Track lists show the language and the audio codec.
- Set the tracks that should start automatically in **Settings → Playback → Default audio language** and **Default subtitle language**. They are applied when playback starts.
- Change how subtitles look in **Settings → Subtitle Appearance**, see [Appearance and language](/docs/usage/appearance-and-theme).

## Playback speed

For movies and series, choose a speed from **0.5×** to **2×** in the **Playback Speed** menu (on Apple TV, the **Speed** tab of the info panel). Live streams always play at normal speed.

## Quality

The **Quality** menu lists the quality variants of a live channel. See [Automatic quality](/docs/usage/live-tv#automatic-quality).

## Info panel

The info panel shows the title's overview and poster, and a **Technical** tab with the current quality, video and audio details.

## Episodes and channels

- Series: **Previous Episode** / **Next Episode**, and an up-next countdown at the end of an episode.
- Live: **Previous Channel** / **Next Channel**.

## Picture in Picture (iPhone and iPad)

Tap the Picture in Picture button to keep watching in a floating window while you use other apps. Picture in Picture works with streams played by the system player. Streams that need the VLC engine do not support it yet.

## AirPlay (iPhone and iPad, Premium)

Send video to an Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible TV from the player. Streams that need the VLC engine cannot send video over AirPlay; Livo tells you when that happens.

## Cast (Android phones and tablets, Premium)

Use the Cast button in the player to play on a Chromecast or Google TV device on the same Wi-Fi network.

## When playback fails

Livo retries a failed stream a few times before showing **Playback error**. Choose **Retry** to try again. See [Playback issues](/docs/troubleshooting/playback-issues).

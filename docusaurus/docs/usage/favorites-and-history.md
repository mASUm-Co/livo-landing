# Continue Watching, favorites and sync

## Continue Watching

Anything you start appears in **Continue Watching** on Home, with your progress. Series show the season and episode, for example *S02 • E05*.

<DocScreenshot shot="tablet-01-home" alt="Continue Watching and Recently Added rows on the iPad home screen" caption="Continue Watching on iPad" />

- Select an item to **Resume**, or choose **Start Over**.
- Long-press an item to **Remove from Continue Watching** or **Mark as watched**.
- **Clear All** empties the row.
- When you close the player after starting from Continue Watching, you return to Home.

Movies and episodes resume from where you stopped, with either playback engine.

## Favorites

Save live channels with a long-press → **Add to Favorites**. They are listed under **Favorites** in Live. Movies and series use the **Watchlist**, see [Movies and series](/docs/usage/movies-and-series).

## Sync across devices (Premium)

- **iPhone, iPad and Apple TV**: with Premium, settings and Continue Watching sync through **iCloud** across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Progress is merged per title, and items you finish or remove stay removed on every device. Use **Sync** in Continue Watching to sync right away.
- **Android**: Premium members can link a Livo account in **Settings → Account** to sync across Android devices. On Android TV, choose **Sign in with phone** and approve the TV from your phone.

With sync on, **Clear All** clears Continue Watching on every connected device.

# Login or credential errors

These apply to Xtream Codes accounts.

- Check the **Server URL**, including `http://` or `https://` and the port, for example `http://host:port`.
- Check the **Username** and **Password** for typos. They are case-sensitive.
- Ask your provider whether the account is active and not expired.
- The provider's server may be temporarily down. Try again later.
- Some providers limit how many devices can be connected at the same time.

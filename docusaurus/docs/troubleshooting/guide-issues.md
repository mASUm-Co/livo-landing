# TV guide is empty or wrong

## No guide at all

- Edit your source in **IPTV Sources** and look at **Program guide**. If it says *No guide address found*, your playlist has no guide address. Enter an XMLTV address from your provider.
- Choose **Refresh guide** and check the status line for errors.
- The guide only refreshes while the source is active.

## Some channels have no guide

The status line shows how many channels matched, for example *280/300 channels matched*. Channels without a match are not in the guide, or their guide id differs. Ask your provider for a guide that covers them.

## Programme times are off

If every programme is off by the same amount, change **Time shift** in the source's **Program guide** settings. Times are shown in your device's time zone.

See [TV guide](/docs/usage/tv-guide).

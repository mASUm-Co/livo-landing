# Playback issues

## A stream does not start

- Try another channel or title from the same source. If nothing plays, the source itself may be down.
- Livo retries a failed stream automatically and falls back to its second engine. If you still see **Playback error**, choose **Retry**.
- Test the same source on another device or app to rule out the provider.
- Many providers allow only one stream at a time. Stop playback on your other devices.

## Buffering or freezing on live TV

- Keep **Automatic Stream Quality** on (**Settings → Playback**). It moves to a lower quality when the stream keeps buffering.
- If you picked a quality by hand, choose a lower one in the **Quality** menu.
- Check your connection speed. 4K streams need a fast, stable connection.

## The player shows a delay on live TV

If you paused or the stream stalled, you are behind live. Choose **Live** in the player to jump back to the live edge.

## No sound, wrong language or no subtitles

- Pick another audio or subtitle track in the player.
- Check **Settings → Playback** for default audio and subtitle languages.
- On Apple TV with HomePod or AirPlay speakers, pause and play once to resync.

## AirPlay or Picture in Picture is not available

Streams that play with the VLC engine do not support AirPlay video or Picture in Picture. AirPlay also requires Premium.
